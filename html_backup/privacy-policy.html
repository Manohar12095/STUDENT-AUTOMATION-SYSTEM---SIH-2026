Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 · Last Updated: September 15, 2026

🛡️ Summary (Plain English)
  • We store face embeddings (math vectors), not your photos
  • Your video never leaves your device — all face processing is on-device
  • You can delete your biometric data at any time from Settings → Privacy
  • We never sell your data to third parties
  • OTP codes are single-use, time-limited, and hashed — never stored as plain text

1. Who We Are

StudentAuth ("we", "our", "us") is an educational identity verification platform operated for academic institutions. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal and biometric data.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Institution, department, roll/register number
  • Preferred languages, gender (optional), address (optional)
  • Password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot read it)

2.2 Biometric Data

  • Face embeddings only — 128-dimensional mathematical vectors derived from your face captures
  • Raw images are processed on your device and immediately discarded after embedding extraction
  • Embeddings are encrypted at rest and tied to your account
  • We record the face-recognition model version used, so embeddings can be migrated if the model is updated

2.3 Session & Activity Data

  • Login timestamps, approximate device/browser info
  • Activity monitor state transitions (Active / Idle / Drowsy) — never raw video
  • OTP request and verification timestamps

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication — verifying your identity when you sign in
  • Activity monitoring — classifying your presence state during sessions (only with your explicit consent)
  • Security — detecting brute-force attempts, rate limiting, and audit logging
  • Communication — sending OTP codes and account notifications to your email

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / PDPB)

  • Consent — biometric data and activity monitoring require your explicit, informed, revocable consent
  • Contractual necessity — account data is required to provide the service
  • Legitimate interests — security logging and fraud prevention

5. Biometric Data — Special Category

Face embeddings are classified as biometric data under applicable privacy law (GDPR Article 9, India PDPB). We treat this data with heightened protection:

  • Encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Access restricted to authenticated systems only
  • Never shared with third parties for any purpose
  • Deleted immediately and permanently upon your request
  • Explicit, separate consent obtained before any collection

6. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained until you delete your account
  • Face embeddings — deleted when you delete your biometric data or account
  • Activity logs — retained for 90 days, then automatically purged
  • Login history — retained for 12 months for security purposes
  • OTP records — consumed or expired OTPs are purged within 24 hours

7. Your Rights

  • Access — view all your stored data (Settings → Profile)
  • Deletion — delete biometric data (Settings → Privacy) or entire account
  • Correction — update profile information at any time
  • Portability — request a copy of your data by contacting us
  • Withdraw consent — revoke biometric or monitor consent anytime from Settings
  • Objection — opt out of non-essential processing

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store session tokens and user state. No third-party tracking cookies are used. Session tokens expire after 8 hours of inactivity.

9. Children's Privacy

This service is intended for students aged 14 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or complaints: privacy@studentauth.example
